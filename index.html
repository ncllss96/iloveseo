About

I build systems that make growth repeatable.

My work is grounded in news media, but the underlying problems are not media-specific: finding demand, building useful products, automating workflows, changing behaviour and scaling what works.

I came to growth through an unconventional route—building a sports community, running e-commerce and working across content, marketing and search. That range is why I am most useful where strategy has to become something people can actually use.

I am based in Switzerland and work comfortably across German, English and French. I care about clear thinking, direct feedback and outcomes that survive beyond the original project team.

01

Tamedia · SEO transformation

Turning SEO from specialist knowledge into newsroom practice

I helped build two SEO teams and led Tamedia’s eight-week “Reach Offensive”. We then institutionalised the initiative as an ongoing cross-functional Reach Team, which I helped conceptualise and build—from roles, responsibilities and workflows to shift design. More than 600 journalists and producers were trained and supported with dashboards and CMS-integrated tools.

Expanding from search optimisation to audience growth

In 2026, I took the lead of Tamedia's newly founded Growth Lab. The team combines editorial expertise, content strategy, data and product thinking to identify audience opportunities, formulate hypotheses, test ideas and transfer what works into daily operations.

03

Tamedia · Briefing Kitchen

Turning fragmented signals into one daily editorial decision layer

Briefing Kitchen began as my idea. I laid its foundation, my team developed it into what it is today, and I remain responsible for it. The AI-assisted Slack briefing serves the Newsdesk and is visible to the wider editorial organisation. It combines Search and Discover performance with NewzDash, Google Trends, Wikipedia, Reddit, YouTube and news-sitemap data, then highlights gaps between audience demand and existing coverage.
